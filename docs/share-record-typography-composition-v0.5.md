# SGM / Share a Record — Typography & Composition v0.5

## Composition

- `Singapore Architecture Map` remains the upper-left brand line.
- `Visited Record` moves beneath the brand and becomes the record title.
- The accent count and `Places visited` share one left-origin information group; label position responds to digit count.
- The coastline and category rings use the unchanged geographic projection, with tighter geographic bounds and a larger projection frame.
- The effective land extent aligns near the 72 px text margin without adding a visible map frame.
- No rules, cards, backgrounds, legends, badges, or decorative elements were added.

## Visible attribution proposal

The bottom line reads:

`National Map Polygon · SLA · accessed 24 Nov 2026 · data.gov.sg/open-data-licence`

This is a visual attribution proposal, not a legal determination. It names the dataset and agency, records the access date, and gives the licence URL in a form that remains visible in a standalone PNG. Whether its size and treatment meet the Singapore Open Data Licence requirement for a conspicuous notice should be confirmed before public release.

## Mobile-size review

Validation images are also reviewed at 360 × 450 px, equivalent to a 1080 × 1350 image displayed at 360 CSS pixels wide. The title, count, label, coastline, and category distribution remain legible; the attribution is intentionally subordinate and is the limiting text size.
