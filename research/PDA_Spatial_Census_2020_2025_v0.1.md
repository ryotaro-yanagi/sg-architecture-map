# SGM / P*DA Architecture and Spatial Works — 2020 & 2025 Pass v0.1

Research date: 2026-10-27. Purpose: independently enumerate each year’s Design of the Year recipients, not confuse Designer of the Year awards with project awards, and distinguish Singapore from overseas sites.

## Inclusion decision

The scope is built architecture and designed spaces situated in Singapore (including spatial interiors, experience-design landscapes and physically realised small pavilions). Each awarded object is counted once. An architect's practice does not make an award-winning overseas building eligible for the Singapore map.
